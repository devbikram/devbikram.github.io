Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated 20 November 2026

This is the personal website of Bikram Keshari Pahi, an independent software engineer based in Bengaluru, India. I am responsible for the personal data described here. This policy explains what I collect when you contact me or book a call, why, who helps me process it, and what you can ask me to do with it.

What I collect

  • Booking a consultation: the details you enter on the Google Calendar booking page, usually your name, email address and the time you choose.
  • Email and phone: whatever you include if you email or call me directly.

This site has no contact form, analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one small item in your own browser and nowhere else: your light or dark theme choice.

Why I use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, discuss and arrange the work you ask about, and keep a record of our conversation. I do not sell your data, add you to a mailing list or use it for marketing.

I rely on your consent, which you give by booking a call or by contacting me yourself. Where data protection law allows it, I also rely on the steps needed before entering into a contract with you. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Who processes it

  • Google provides my email (Gmail) and the Google Calendar booking page. Booking details are also handled under Google's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Some of these providers store data outside India, including in the United States.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact, or for as long as we are working together, then delete them. If we sign a contract, records needed for tax and legal purposes are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR, you can ask me to:

  • tell you what personal data I hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct or complete it;
  • delete it, or stop using it;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you.

To do any of these, email me. I will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with my answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If I change how I handle personal data, I will update this page and the date at the top.
